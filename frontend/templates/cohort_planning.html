1 Set a target class average — the optimiser searches for a weight combination that puts the class as close as possible to it.
2 For every assessment, choose a range: the minimum and maximum weight it may carry. These ranges are the constraints of the optimisation.
3 No assessment may be weighted to zero. Minimums must stay above {{ min_weight_pct }}% — the solver enforces a {{ min_weight_pct }}% floor even if you type 0.
4 The weights must add up to 100%, so the minimums may not sum to more than 100%, and the maximums may not sum to less than 100%. The page checks this live and explains any problem before you calculate.
5 Press Calculate weights. The result shows the proposed spread, the range each weight was allowed to move in, and whether the solver reached your target.
6 If the target is out of reach within the ranges, the solver still returns the closest achievable set and tells you how far off it is — widen the ranges or lower the target to close the gap.
